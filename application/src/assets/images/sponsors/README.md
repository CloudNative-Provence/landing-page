# Sponsor assets

Only sponsors in the supplied 2026 roster are displayed: one Platinum, four Gold, three Silver and six Community sponsors. Tier assignments and site links live in `domains/event/sponsors/sponsors.ts`.

Cards stay compact with a single action: “Découvrir [sponsor]” / “Discover [sponsor]” opens the complete localized pitch in a native dialog, which includes the sponsor’s site link. Every sponsor must have a pitch in French and English; the `Sponsor` type requires both. Without JavaScript, pitches and site links remain available in expandable details.

Monaco Digital is the confirmed Platinum sponsor and links to `https://monacodigital.mc/`. Its [official vector logo](https://monacodigital.mc/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Fichier-1.svg) uses red and white lettering, displayed on dark card and dialog backgrounds. The French and English pitch is drafted from [Monaco Digital's company overview](https://monacodigital.mc/a-propos/).

The Red Hat logo comes from the supplied sponsor archive. Its reverse variant is used on the dark Gold card and dialog backgrounds. The French and English pitch is drafted from [Red Hat's company overview](https://www.redhat.com/en/about).

Teleport is the public Silver sponsor represented by the roster's ZenOps entry, as confirmed by the organizer, and links to `https://goteleport.com/`. Its logo and English pitch come from the supplied `zenops - teleport` folder; the French pitch is a translation of the document's long version.
